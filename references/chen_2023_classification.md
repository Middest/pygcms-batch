# Chen 2023 Classification Scheme

Reference: Chen et al. 2023, "Biochemical composition of soil organic matter physical fractions under 32-year fertilization in Ferralic Cambisol", Carbon Research 2:1. DOI: 10.1007/s44246-022-00034-0

## 10 Chemical Groups

| Group | Description | Typical compounds |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| Lipids | Alkanes, alkenes, fatty acids, esters, alcohols, ketones, aldehydes, sterols, terpenes | n-alkanes (C10-C40), fatty acid methyl esters, long-chain alcohols |
| Monocyclic aromatics | Single-ring aromatic hydrocarbons | Benzene, toluene, xylene, ethylbenzene, styrene, phenol, cresol |
| Polycyclic aromatics | Multi-ring aromatic hydrocarbons | Naphthalene, anthracene, phenanthrene, pyrene, fluoranthene, biphenyl |
| Phenolics | Phenolic compounds | Guaiacol, syringol, vanillin, catechol, eugenol, hydroxybenzoic acids |
| Polysaccharides | Sugar derivatives, furans, pyrans | Levoglucosan, furfural, furanones, pyranones, anhydro-sugars |
| Lignins | Lignin monomers and derivatives | Guaiacyl, syringyl, hydroxyphenyl units, coniferyl/sinapyl alcohols |
| Amino N-bearing | N-containing from amino acid pyrolysis | Nitriles, pyridines, pyrroles, pyrazines, imidazoles, indoles |
| Heterocyclic N-bearing | Other N-heterocycles | Pyrazoles, oxazoles, thiazoles, triazoles, benzimidazoles, purines |
| Other N-bearing | Other N-containing compounds | Nitro, nitroso, amines, amides, ammonium, urea, guanidine derivatives |
| Unspecified | Halogen/Si/P/S-containing, unknown | Chlorinated compounds, siloxanes, phosphates, sulfones, unknowns |

## Source Attribution

### Plant-derived
- Lignins
- Long-chain lipids (>18C)
- Polysaccharides
- Phenolics
- Amino N-bearing: indoles only
- Heterocyclic N-compounds: pyrazines only

### Microbial-derived
- Aromatics (monocyclic + polycyclic)
- Short-chain lipids (<16C)
- Amino N-bearing: nitriles, pyridines, pyrroles (NOT indoles)
- Other N-bearing compounds

### Mixed (plant + microbial)
- Unspecified compounds
- Lipids with 16C-18C chain length
- Compounds with multiple possible origins
- Halogen/Si/P/S-containing compounds
